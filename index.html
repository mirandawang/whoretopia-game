a small game about whores, community, and the fight.

welcome to your cozy street. you're a sex worker making your living. you like what you do and you're building your life, but the laws are stacked against you: FOSTA-SESTA shut-down screening platforms, frozen payment accounts, criminalizing safety chats between workers. this game asks what if we can fight back and build our own communities?

over 10 days, you'll rest, take bookings, visit your lovely neighbors, and fight the law with solidarity and wellness.

every win unlocks a perk, more safety, and a new neighbor moves in. by day 10, your neighborhood could look like a whoretopia. sweet. sexy. built by whores.

this is what it could look like. let's build it together!
