whoretopia

a small game about whores, community, and the fight.

welcome to your cozy street. you're a sex worker making your living. you do the work and you're good at it. but the laws are stacked against you: shut-down screening platforms, frozen payment accounts, criminalizing safety chats between workers. this game asks what if we can fight back and build our own communities?

over 10 days, you'll rest, take bookings, visit your lovely neighbors, and fight the law with solidarity and wellness.

every win unlocks a perk, more safety, and a new neighbor moves in. by day 10, your street could look like a whoretopia. soft. sexy. built by workers.

this is what it could look like. let's build it together!
